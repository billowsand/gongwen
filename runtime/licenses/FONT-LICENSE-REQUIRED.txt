The document and research fonts under runtime/fonts are deployment assets.
Their OpenType embedding flags permit the tested PDF embedding modes, but that
does not itself grant permission to redistribute the original font files.

Before distributing a portable package, the distributor must verify that its
font licenses permit software redistribution and PDF embedding in the intended
deployment environment.

JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; see
JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt. TeX Gyre Termes is distributed under the GUST Font
License; see TeX-Gyre-GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt.

The FZ (Founder) fonts have no redistribution grant of their own; clearing them
for the intended deployment is part of the check described above.
